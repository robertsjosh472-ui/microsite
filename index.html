The problem

Would you still understand this tenancy two years from now?

A reference is completed. An agreement is signed. A gas safety certificate is served. The keys are handed over.

Two years later the rent slips, a claim is challenged or the landlord wants the property back. The documents may still exist — but what they prove may no longer be clear.

Disconnected systems, repeated data entry and endless chasing make it harder to move a tenancy forward, and harder still to stand behind it later.
